Our Story

Ruslan Orlinsky

Creative Director & Founder · FLORINSKY Atelier

FLORINSKY is a premium floral atelier founded by Ruslan Orlinsky, a professional wedding photographer with 19 years of industry experience. You can explore his photography portfolio at www.orlinsky.ca.

Through years of working across hundreds of weddings and events, one thing became crystal clear: clients don't just want beautiful decor — they demand professional service and the absolute peace of mind that everything will be executed flawlessly.

That is exactly why FLORINSKY was born. We built an atelier where client care and a premium service standard are just as foundational to our philosophy as the beauty of our floral walls.

We have introduced a suite of solutions that are virtually unmatched in the Canadian market:

  • Ultra-realistic "Real Touch" flowers that look and feel alive, even up close.
  • Grand, oversized wall dimensions for maximum visual impact.
  • Reinforced structures engineered for ultimate safety.
  • All-terrain adaptability, allowing for secure installation on uneven ground.
  • An uncompromised standard of reliability at every single stage of our collaboration.

We seamlessly blend premium decor, thoughtful engineering, and high-end client service. Our floral walls are so lifelike that guests frequently walk up just to try and catch a scent.

Our goal is simple: to let our clients truly enjoy their celebration while we handle the details.
