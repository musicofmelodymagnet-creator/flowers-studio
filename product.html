Engineering Details

01

Three Size Options

Available in 8 × 8, 11 × 8, and 14 × 8 feet — every event finds its perfect scale. Each configuration delivers the same quality of craftsmanship and structural integrity, adapting seamlessly to intimate gatherings and grand celebrations alike.

02

Seamless Support System

All structural supports are fully concealed behind the wall — the presentation remains immaculate from every angle. Guests see only the flowers, never the engineering that holds them.

03

Reinforced Stability

Oversized base supports with precision anti-tipping engineering. Built to remain perfectly stable even when large groups lean in for photos, ensuring total peace of mind throughout your event.

04

All-Terrain Installation

An adjustable leg system enables secure installation on uneven ground, grass, pavement, and hillside venues. Where other walls require a perfectly level surface, ours adapts to the real world.

05

Semi-Circular Configuration

The frame can be shaped into a graceful semi-circle, creating an enveloping backdrop that wraps around your guests. An immersive visual experience that flat walls simply cannot achieve.
