# PRODUCT.md — FLORINSKY Atelier

## Register
brand

## Product
Premium flower wall rental studio serving Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area. Founded by Ruslan Orlinsky, professional wedding photographer with 19 years of experience. Real Touch floral materials, aerospace-grade frames, triple-refund guarantee.

## Target Users
- Engaged couples planning Toronto/GTA weddings
- Event planners and corporate event coordinators
- Individuals hosting milestone celebrations (baby showers, birthdays, engagement parties)

## Brand Personality
Three words: **serene, precise, luxurious.** Physical object: a Parisian atelier sample card — ivory linen, debossed letterpress, single lilac ribbon. Calm like a florist studio on a quiet Tuesday morning. Assured like an artisan who charges what they charge.

## Anti-references
- Rustic barn wedding boards (Pinterest-beige, mason jars)
- Loud event rental sites with neon pricing grids
- Generic e-commerce flower delivery
- Over-rounded "cute" app aesthetics

## Design Principles
1. Identity-preservation: committed to existing brand design system (neumorphic rose-cream, --brand #A878D0, Playfair Display wordmark + Plus Jakarta Sans body)
2. Spatial precision: every element placed with intent, no accidental alignment
3. Calm authority: no urgency cues, no countdown timers, no "act now"
4. Typography carries brand weight: generous leading, controlled hierarchy

## Site Structure
Static HTML multi-page site. Pages: index, gallery, product, advantages, kind-words, about, faq, client-care, privacy-policy, terms-of-use, locations (new).
