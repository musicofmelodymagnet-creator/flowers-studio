Dramatic Depth for Weddings & Events Across Toronto & the GTA

Midnight Bloom is our darkest, most opulent backdrop — a burgundy flower wall layered with deep wine roses, plum orchids, dusty-rose blooms, and gilded gold calla lilies. It turns a plain venue corner into the centrepiece of the room, and it is one of the only dark moody flower walls available to rent across Toronto & the GTA.

  • Size: 8×8 ft (extendable to 11×8 ft)
  • Material: Real Touch (latex / PU) petals — lifelike up close
  • Palette: Deep burgundy, wine, plum, dusty rose, gold accents
  • Includes: Delivery, professional setup & takedown
  • Best for: Weddings, birthdays, engagement parties, corporate events
  • Coverage: Toronto & the Greater Toronto Area

Every bloom is Real Touch — latex and PU petals that look and feel like fresh flowers, without the shine or fabric texture of silk. The wall stands 8×8 ft and extends to 11×8 ft for larger group photos, arriving fully assembled with professional setup and takedown. Warm uplighting deepens the red rose tones and gold, making it especially striking for evening weddings and receptions. For styling inspiration, see burgundy styling ideas in our Flower Wall Guide.

Built on our reinforced frame with adjustable feet, Midnight Bloom holds steady indoors and outdoors, on level floors or uneven ground. Every FLORINSKY rental is backed by our triple delivery guarantee. Preview the exact wall in 3D before you book.
