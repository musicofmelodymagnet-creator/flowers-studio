The perfect solution for large groups of guests

Don't make your guests squeeze into the frame. The classic 8×8 ft floral wall format is ideal for small groups, but for large-scale events it simply isn't enough. That is why we developed an expanded 11×8 ft size — it allows larger groups to take photos without stepping outside a beautifully composed scene.

At events, guests often take group photos — families, friends, colleagues, or larger groups who want to capture memories together.

When a standard 8×8 ft flower wall is used, people positioned at the edges of the frame inevitably extend beyond the floral backdrop and end up standing against plain walls or other venue elements. As a result, the photos look less impressive and lose the sense of a fully luxurious setting.

To avoid this, we have developed not only the standard 8×8 ft format, but also an expanded 11×8 ft flower wall size. The additional width allows more guests to be comfortably included within a beautiful floral space, making group photos look more balanced, cohesive, and professionally composed.

Don't let the venue's terrain limit your ideas

Traditionally, flower walls can only be installed on perfectly level surfaces. However, thanks to our adjustable leg tilt system, our walls can also be set up on slopes and other uneven terrain. This allows you to choose your location based on its beauty, not the limitations of the flower wall setup.

Some of the most beautiful event locations are outdoors in nature. However, many flower walls are designed to be installed only on perfectly level surfaces, which significantly limits location options. As a result, clients are often forced to give up ideal venues or look for compromises.

We have solved this issue with an adjustable leg angle system for our stands. This allows flower walls to be securely installed even on hills and other uneven terrain. Now, when choosing your event location, you can focus on the beauty of the scenery rather than technical limitations.

This gives you greater freedom in bringing your ideas to life and the ability to create truly stunning photos in the most beautiful settings.
