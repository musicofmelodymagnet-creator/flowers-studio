Venue Access for Flower Wall Setup

Access is one of the most useful details to settle before booking a flower wall. Toronto venues range from large event complexes to hotels, restaurants, banquet rooms and smaller private spaces, and the easiest route for guests is not always the route used by vendors.

Ask the venue or event coordinator to confirm:

  • the exact room or event space, not only the venue name;
  • the service or vendor entrance;
  • where unloading is permitted and whether a loading window is required;
  • whether the carrying route includes stairs, elevators, narrow corridors or outdoor sections;
  • when the space is available for setup;
  • how access works when the flower wall is removed after the event.

These details help avoid last-minute decisions at the door. They are especially important when a room has a known access feature, such as the stair access published for Old Mill Toronto's Drawing Room.

Outdoor Flower Walls: Venue Courtyards and City Parks

Private Venue Courtyards

A private courtyard can be a useful location for a flower wall when the venue permits décor there. Before booking, confirm that the selected courtyard or patio is included with your event space, that external décor is allowed and that there is an approved route for setup and removal.

It is also sensible to identify an indoor backup location with the venue in case the outdoor plan changes. The technical suitability of a specific FLORINSKY wall for a particular outdoor location should be confirmed as part of the rental discussion rather than assumed from the venue alone.

Wedding Ceremonies in City Parks

City park rules are different from private-venue rules. For standard City of Toronto wedding ceremony bookings in parks, the City currently states that chairs, furniture, decorations and props are not allowed.

That means a flower wall should not be assumed to be permitted simply because a park wedding ceremony has been booked. If your event uses a different type of permit or a different City facility, confirm the applicable rules with the City before making décor plans.
